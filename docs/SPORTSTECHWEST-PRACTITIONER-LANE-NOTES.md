## The question

Could SportsTechWest run a lane that builds decision training tools *with* practitioners, for trainers and players across all sports, rather than shipping one basketball product?

## The working thesis

SportsTechWest is not "BallCODE for other sports." It is the house that builds decision training tools with practitioners, one sport at a time. BallCODE is instance one.

What the product actually sells is not basketball. It is preparation attached to performance: an athlete trained to read a cue, make the call, and see immediately whether the read was right. That mechanism is sport agnostic. The basketball court is the board, not the point.

**What transfers between sports:** the method, the engine, and the trainer relationships.
**What does not transfer:** the cue vocabulary, the read and react logic, the playtesting. Each sport is a rebuild, not a reskin. This is the single most important line in this file.

**Why build *with* practitioners rather than sell *to* them:** a trainer who co-designed the cue vocabulary brings their own athletes and does not churn. A trainer who was sold a subscription does. The co-build is the moat.

## The gate

**Do not open a second sport until:** one trainer has run BallCODE basketball with their own athletes twice, and asked for it again unprompted.

That single fact is what makes the SportsTechWest pitch true instead of aspirational. Until then, every trainer conversation stays about basketball.

**Why the gate is set here:** as of 2026-09-08 the evidence is one family signed up and one interested trainer. Widening at that stage produces four half products and proof in none of them. The gate costs nothing to hold and everything to skip.

## Sequencing note

This lane sits behind the existing GTM order, it does not replace it. Players first via youth tournaments, then trainers, then coaches inbound. This lane is what trainers become *after* they are already in, not a new front door.
