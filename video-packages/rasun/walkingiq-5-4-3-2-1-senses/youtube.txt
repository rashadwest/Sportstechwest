1 Hour 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Walk: Anchor Yourself With Your Senses | walkingIQ | Sportstechwest

walkingIQ: a one-hour guided walk built on the 5-4-3-2-1 grounding technique: name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell and 1 you taste.

Your senses only work in the present. Use them on purpose, and your mind comes back to the moment with them.

The walk:
• Learn the 5-4-3-2-1 round
• Rounds two and three: find what you missed, then slow it down to one breath per sense
• Quiet stretches: music only, start a round on your own whenever your mind drifts
• Short version for game day: one thing you see, one you hear, next play
• Cool-down and one last round

Short affirmations along the way: I am here. Right now. That's enough.

Walk somewhere safe, with your eyes up and one ear open.

Why it works: 5-4-3-2-1 is a grounding exercise used widely by coaches and counselors. Naming what you sense pulls your attention out of your head and into the present. It's a focus tool, not a treatment. If stress or anxiety is ongoing, talk to a professional.
