# walkingIQ — 5-4-3-2-1 Senses (1-hour guided walk)
# Anchor with the senses: 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste. Repeat in rounds.
# One segment per paragraph, 5 seconds of quiet after each. "[walk N]" = music-only stretch (scaled to fill 60:00).

This is walkingIQ. One hour. One simple way to get out of your head and back into the moment. Five, four, three, two, one.

Walk somewhere safe. Keep your eyes up and one ear open to what's around you.

Here's the practice. Name five things you can see. Four things you can touch. Three things you can hear. Two things you can smell. One thing you can taste.

It's called grounding. Your senses only work in the present, so when you use them on purpose, your mind comes with them.

First, just walk. Easy pace. Let your breathing settle on its own.
[walk 45]

Round one. Five things you can see. Say them in your head, slowly. A tree. A car. A crack in the sidewalk. Take your time.
[walk 45]

Four things you can touch. Your feet in your shoes. The air on your hands. The fabric of your shirt. Your phone in your pocket. Notice each one.
[walk 45]

Three things you can hear. Near and far. Your footsteps. A bird. Traffic. Wind.
[walk 40]

Two things you can smell. Cut grass, rain, coffee, your own jacket. If nothing stands out, breathe in through your nose and notice what's there.
[walk 40]

One thing you can taste. Take a sip of water, or just notice the taste already in your mouth.
[walk 40]

That's one round. Notice how much quieter it got up there.

I am here. Right now. That's enough.
[walk 75]

Keep walking. Let the pace find you. Your breath, your steps, the world going by.
[walk 90]

Round two. This time, find things you missed the first time. Five new things you can see. Look high. Look low.
[walk 50]

Four new things to touch. The swing of your arms. The weight of each step. The temperature of the air.
[walk 45]

Three sounds. See if you can find one that's very quiet. Something you'd usually tune out.
[walk 40]

Two smells. One thing to taste.
[walk 45]

I notice more when I slow down.
[walk 90]

Use this anytime your mind starts racing. Before a game. Before a meeting. When you feel the spiral starting. Five, four, three, two, one.

You don't have to fix the thought. You just have to give your attention somewhere better to go.
[walk 90]

My senses bring me home.
[walk 90]

Round three. Slower this time. Spend a full breath on each thing you name.

Five things you see. Breathe with each one.
[walk 60]

Four things you touch. Three you hear.
[walk 60]

Two you smell. One you taste.
[walk 45]

I am calm. I am present. I am steady.
[walk 120]

Long quiet stretch now. If your mind wanders, start a round on your own. Five things you see.
[walk 150]

Still with me. Good.

Notice the colors around you. Pick one color and find five things that are that color.
[walk 90]

I don't need to be anywhere else.
[walk 120]

Round four, your pace. Five, four, three, two, one. Go through it on your own.
[walk 120]

Grounding isn't about a perfect walk. It's about coming back, again and again, to what's real.

I come back to the moment. Every time.
[walk 120]

Start to slow down. Easier steps. Longer exhales.
[walk 75]

One last round, short and sweet. One thing you see. One thing you touch. One thing you hear. One thing you smell. One thing you taste.
[walk 60]

I am here. Right now. That's enough.

That's walkingIQ. Five, four, three, two, one. Take it on your next walk, or into your next hard moment.
