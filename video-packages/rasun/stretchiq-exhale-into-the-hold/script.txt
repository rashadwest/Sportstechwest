## Warm-up
This is stretchIQ. One hour. Soft stretch. Steady breath.

Find a place you can stretch without forcing it.

Keep one ear open to your body. Ease off if anything sharp shows up.

The whole idea is simple.

In for three. Out for four.

In soft. Out long. The exhale opens the hold.

Start easy. No deep stretch yet.

## Build
Pick one stretch you know.

Settle into it. Do not bounce.

In for three. Out for four.

[quiet 90]

If the stretch fights you, back off a little.

In for three. Out for four.
