runningIQ: a one-hour guided run that starts with one intention and keeps coming back to it: Light feet. Grateful heart.

Gratitude isn't just a feeling on a run. It's a focus strategy. It moves your attention from what hurts to what's working: your breath, your legs, the air, the sky, the people who got you here.

The run:
• Set your intention before the pace picks up
• Easy miles: one thing in nature you're thankful for
• Middle miles: run a stretch for someone who helped you get here
• Quiet stretches: music only, say the mantra in time with your steps
• Cool-down: look back at the intention you set

Released on Thanksgiving. Happy Thanksgiving from Sportstechwest.

Run somewhere safe and keep one ear open to traffic and the world around you.

Why it works: people who regularly wrote down what they were grateful for reported better well-being than people who listed hassles (Emmons & McCullough, 2003). And motivational self-talk helped cyclists last longer at a hard effort while it felt easier (Blanchfield et al., 2014). A mantra is self-talk you chose on purpose.
