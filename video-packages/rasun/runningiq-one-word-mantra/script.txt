# runningIQ: One Word Mantra (1-hour guided run, week 2)
# Same idea: start with an intention, carry gratitude for nature, repeat the mantra all run. This time the mantra is one word you choose.
# One segment per paragraph, 5 seconds of quiet after each. "[run N]" = music-only stretch (scaled to fill 60:00).

## Choose your word
This is runningIQ. Last week our intention was light feet, grateful heart. This week, you pick one word, and you run with it for an hour.

Run somewhere safe. One ear open. Eyes up.

Choose your word before you start. Here are a few. Strong. Smooth. Free. Steady. Thankful. Or one that's only yours.

Got it? Say it once in your head. That word is your intention for this run.

Start easy. Walk or jog. Let the body wake up.
[run 60]

Every time I say "your word," say it back in your head. Ready? Your word.
[run 45]

One word works because it's short enough to hold onto when you're tired. A sentence falls apart at mile three. A word doesn't.
[run 75]

Bring the pace up a little. Comfortable. Conversational.

Your word.
[run 90]

## Match it to your footsteps
Try matching it to your footsteps. Say it on every left foot. Left. Word. Left. Word.
[run 90]

Now look around. Find one thing outside that matches your word. If your word is steady, find something steady. A mountain, a wall, a tree trunk.
[run 90]

Your word.
[run 90]

Nature doesn't rush and it doesn't quit. Rivers don't sprint. They just keep going. Let your run feel like that.
[run 90]

## Nature match
Breathe in the air. Notice the temperature, the smell, the light. Be thankful you're out in it.

Your word.
[run 90]

## When it gets hard
When the run gets hard, shrink the world down to the word. Not the miles left. Not the hill ahead. Just the word, on the next step.
[run 120]

Your word.
[run 90]

Check in on your body. Shoulders. Jaw. Hands. Let them go loose. Run tall.
[run 90]

Your word.
[run 120]

If you picked a word that isn't working today, you're allowed to change it. Pick the one you need right now.
[run 90]

Your word.
[run 120]

## Quiet stretches and cool-down
Long quiet stretch. Just you, the road, and your word, over and over.
[run 150]

Your word.
[run 120]

Notice one thing in nature you've never paid attention to on this route. The shape of a cloud. The sound of the leaves. Thank it.
[run 120]

Your word.
[run 120]

Start to ease the pace down. Let the breath settle.
[run 75]

This word goes with you after the run. Before a game, a test, a hard conversation. One breath, one word.

Your word.
[run 60]

Walk it out. Breathe deep. Look around one more time.
[run 45]

That's runningIQ. One word, again and again. Take it into your next run.
