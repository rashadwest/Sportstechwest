1 Hour Reset Walk: 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding After a Hard Day | walkingIQ | Sportstechwest

walkingIQ: week two. The same 5-4-3-2-1 grounding technique, aimed at the moments athletes really need it: after a long day, a tough practice or a game that didn't go your way.

Name what you're carrying, set it down, and let your senses bring you back: 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.

The walk:
• Let the day out, then slow it down
• Rounds that get more specific: the shape of one leaf, the quietest sound you can find
• The athlete's short version: one thing you see, one you hear, next play
• Long quiet stretches with music
• Cool-down: notice how different you feel from the start

Short affirmations along the way: I am more than one moment. Next play. Next step. Next breath.

Walk somewhere safe, with your eyes up and one ear open.

Why it works: 5-4-3-2-1 is a grounding exercise used widely by coaches and counselors. Naming what you sense gives your attention somewhere better to go than the replay. It's a focus tool, not a treatment. If stress or anxiety is ongoing, talk to a professional.
