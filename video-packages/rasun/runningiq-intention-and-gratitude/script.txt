# runningIQ: Intention & Gratitude (1-hour guided run). Scheduled for Thanksgiving.
# Start with a positive intention / mantra, cultivate gratitude for nature, then come back to the mantra again and again.
# Mantra: "Light feet. Grateful heart." One segment per paragraph, 5 seconds of quiet after each. "[run N]" = music-only stretch.

## Set your intention
This is runningIQ. One hour. One intention. Light feet. Grateful heart.

Run somewhere safe. Keep one ear open to traffic, bikes, and the world around you.

Before you pick up the pace, set your intention for this run. Not a split. Not a distance. A way you want to feel.

Here's ours for today. Light feet. Grateful heart. Say it in your head once.

That's your mantra. Every time you hear it from me, say it back in your head. When your mind drifts, it's your way home.

Start easy. Walk or jog. Let the body warm up before you ask anything of it.
[run 60]

Light feet. Grateful heart.
[run 45]

Gratitude starts small. You get to move today. Your lungs work. Your legs carry you. Not everyone gets that, and not every day.
[run 75]

Let the pace come up a little. Easy enough that you could still talk.

Light feet. Grateful heart.
[run 90]

## Easy miles
Look around. Name one thing in nature you're thankful for right now. The sky. A tree. The cold air. The light.
[run 90]

Light feet means quick, quiet steps. Land under your hips. Let the ground spring you forward.

Light feet. Grateful heart.
[run 90]

Breathe in the air around you like it's a gift. Because it is. Out long and easy.
[run 90]

When the run gets hard, gratitude is a strategy, not just a feeling. It shifts your focus from what hurts to what's working.

Light feet. Grateful heart.
[run 90]

Find something that's working right now. Your arms swinging. Your breath finding a rhythm. Say thank you to it.
[run 90]

Light feet. Grateful heart.
[run 90]

## Middle miles
Think of one person who helped you get here. A coach. A parent. A teammate. Run the next stretch for them.
[run 120]

Light feet. Grateful heart.
[run 90]

Check your form. Shoulders down. Hands loose. Eyes forward. Light feet.
[run 90]

Notice the ground under you. Road, trail, track. It's holding you up, mile after mile.

Light feet. Grateful heart.
[run 120]

If your mind has wandered off to your to-do list, no problem. Come back. Light feet. Grateful heart.
[run 120]

## Quiet stretches
Long quiet stretch now. Say the mantra on your own. Match it to your steps if you like. Light, feet. Grate, ful, heart.
[run 150]

Light feet. Grateful heart.
[run 120]

Look up for a moment. Whatever the sky looks like today, it's the only one like it you'll ever see.
[run 120]

Light feet. Grateful heart.

## Cool-down
Start to ease the pace down. Let your breath come back to easy.
[run 75]

Before you finish, look back at the intention you set. Did you run light? Did you run grateful? Even a little counts.

Light feet. Grateful heart.
[run 60]

Walk it out. Hands on your head or loose at your sides. Breathe it all in.
[run 45]

That's runningIQ. Set an intention. Come back to it again and again. Take it into your next run, and into your day.
