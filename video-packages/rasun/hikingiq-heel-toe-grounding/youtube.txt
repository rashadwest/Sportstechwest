1 Hour Grounding Hike: Heel-Toe Breathing, 3 In 4 Out | hikingIQ | Sportstechwest

hikingIQ: a one-hour guided hike built on one grounding practice: land every step heel first, roll through to the toe, and breathe in over 3 steps, out over 4.

The longer exhale tells your body to settle. The heel-toe step keeps your attention in your feet and on the trail, not in your head.

The hike:
• Warm-up: find the heel-toe step and the 3-in, 4-out count
• Grounding: notice the ground, the air and the sounds around you
• Terrain: shorter steps uphill, soft knees downhill, same rhythm
• Quiet stretches: music only, just you, the trail and the count
• Cool-down: longer exhales to bring your heart rate down

Short affirmations along the way: I am here. I am grounded. Every step brings me back.

Hike somewhere you know, or with someone who does, and keep one ear open to the trail.

Why it works: breathing naturally syncs with the rhythm of your steps (Bramble & Carrier, 1983). The longer exhale over 4 steps keeps your attention on the trail and the count.
