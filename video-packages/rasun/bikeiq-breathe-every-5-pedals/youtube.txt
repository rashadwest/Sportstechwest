1 Hour Breathing Ride: Breathe Every 5 Pedals | bikeIQ | Sportstechwest

bikeIQ: a one-hour guided ride built on one rhythm: breathe in for 5 pedals, breathe out for 5 pedals.

Count one pedal every time your right foot reaches the bottom. Your legs keep the time, and your breath rides on it. Steady breathing settles your nervous system, keeps effort under control on climbs, and speeds up recovery when the road flattens out.

The ride:
• Warm-up: learn the count and lock in the rhythm
• Build: add pressure, keep the breath on beat
• Climb: same rhythm under load (drop to 4 and 4 if you need to, then come back)
• Cruise: long quiet blocks, just you, the pedals and the count
• Cool-down: slow the breath and bring your heart rate down on purpose

Short affirmations along the way, with music-only stretches so you can ride and breathe without talking in your ear.

Ride somewhere safe: an indoor trainer, a bike path or a quiet road, with one ear open to what's around you.

Why it works: breathing naturally locks onto the rhythm of movement (Bramble & Carrier, 1983). At about 80 pedal strokes a minute, 5 in and 5 out works out to roughly 8 breaths a minute, inside the slow-breathing range that research links to more relaxation and less anxiety (Zaccaro et al., 2018).
