1 Hour Swim Session: Breathe Every 5 Strokes | swimIQ | Sports Tech West

swimIQ: a one-hour guided swim built on one rhythm: breathe in for five strokes, breathe out for five strokes.

Count one stroke each time a hand enters the water. Your arms keep the time, and your breath rides on it.

The session:
• Warm-up: learn the count and settle the stroke
• Build: add a little pace, keep five and five
• Lengths: wall to wall on the same rhythm
• Cruise: long quiet swimming, just the count
• Cool-down: ease off and let the counting go

Swim somewhere safe. Stop if you need air. If five strokes feels long, drop to three and come back.

Why it works: matching breath to movement keeps effort steady. A fixed stroke count is a simple metronome for the nervous system in the water.
