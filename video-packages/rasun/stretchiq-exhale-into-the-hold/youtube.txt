1 Hour Stretch Session: Exhale Into the Hold | stretchIQ | Sports Tech West

stretchIQ: a one-hour guided stretch built on one rhythm: breathe in for three, breathe out for four, and soften into the hold on the exhale.

Find a stretch you know. Settle. Do not bounce. On each long out breath, ease a little deeper only if it still feels safe.

The session:
• Warm-up: learn the count and drop the shoulders
• Build: one stretch, breath leads, one percent deeper on the out
• Hold: a second position with the same rule
• Cruise: long quiet hold, just the count
• Cool-down: ease out and let the counting go

Stretch somewhere you can move without forcing it. Stop if anything feels sharp.

Why it works: a longer exhale helps the body settle into a stretch. Matching breath to the hold keeps effort under control instead of yanking into range.
