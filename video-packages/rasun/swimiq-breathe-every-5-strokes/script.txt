# swimIQ: Breathe Every 5 Strokes (1-hour guided swim)
# Short snips (~5 seconds). Identical cues reuse one voice take.
# [quiet N] = music-only swim time. ## = YouTube chapter (not spoken).

## Warm-up
This is swimIQ. One hour. One rhythm. Breathe every five strokes.

Swim somewhere safe. Pool or open water you know.

Keep one ear open to your body. Stop if you need air.

Count one stroke each time a hand enters the water.

In for five strokes. Out for five strokes.

That is the whole session.

Start easy. No race pace yet.
[quiet 45]

In for five strokes. Out for five strokes.

Smooth hands. Soft kick. Steady head.
[quiet 60]

I breathe on rhythm. I swim on rhythm.
[quiet 60]

## Build
Add a little pace. Keep the same count.

In for five strokes. Out for five strokes.
[quiet 90]

If five feels long, go to three. Come back when you can.
[quiet 75]

The breath wants to rush. Do not let it.

In for five strokes. Out for five strokes.
[quiet 90]

I control the pace. The pace does not control me.
[quiet 75]

## Lengths
Picture a long length. Same count wall to wall.

In for five strokes. Out for five strokes.
[quiet 120]

At the wall, reset. Push off. Pick the count back up at one.
[quiet 75]

I keep my rhythm when it gets hard.
[quiet 90]

In for five strokes. Out for five strokes.
[quiet 120]

I recover on purpose.
[quiet 75]

## Cruise
Easy pressure. Just the strokes and the breath.

In for five strokes. Out for five strokes.
[quiet 150]

Notice the water on your skin. The sound of your kick.
[quiet 90]

In for five strokes. Out for five strokes.
[quiet 120]

I finish what I start.
[quiet 90]

## Cool-down
Ease off. Light kick. Soft hands.

In for five strokes. Out for five strokes.
[quiet 60]

Let the counting go. Float or stand easy for a moment.
[quiet 45]

That's swimIQ. Breathe every five strokes.
