# BTE Moves | New rule

This is a post about a new offensive move I have seen that should be examined.

I love the game of basketball. I see a new rule that needs to be implemented for fouls. Steph Curry is one of my favorite players because he is what I envisioned as the way I would play the game of basketball. With that being said, he has mastered the art of the jump shot. Here is the one and only issue I have with Steph. He is so good at shooting and is so smart that he has learned a secret weapon. If he jumps up for a jump shot, he knows someone is normally jumping in his direction with a hard closeout. So Steph, knowing this, has practiced off-balance shots that make sense for him to get his shot off with small windows of opportunity. His best trick is his ability to flail his body while elevating for a jump shot. He normally does this by jumping and changing his shot into more of a push shot and putting his lower body into the defenders’ targeted space to contest. I do not even think it is on purpose; he is just trying to get his shot off most of the time and stops right where the defensive player is going and anticipates the contact. We do not perceive him as flopping because it is more like an anticipation of contact and changing his shot because he thinks he may get fouled by stopping in the defender's path to pull up for a shot.

You cannot jump into their space, so they can land.

You cannot touch any part of their body while they are shooting.

You can even foul them after they release the ball.

I also think it is not a rule when shooting a jump shot, but only when going to the basket. So, how would it be a flop while suspended in the air? I think we should look at it like someone stopping in the lane to get the foul. I think that the fact that Steph Curry has perfected shooting while distorting his body in the air to anticipate a defender colliding with him because he is in his line of movement is just crazy. Meaning he is the best shooter of all time, no doubt haha. Similar to players who have to use their off-hand when going to the basket to shield and push off the defender or players who stop and jump back into the defender, this should be looked at. As the NBA has become a 3-point league over the past couple of years, it is important to develop the rules with the game. I am not taking anything away from Steph. Just like today's rules exist because Wilt, Shaq, Jordan, etc. made the league say this person has figured something out about the game that gives them a unique advantage. I think Steph has found that thing.
