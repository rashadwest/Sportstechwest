1 Hour One Word Mantra Run: Pick Your Word, Run With It | runningIQ | Sportstechwest

runningIQ: week two. Last week the intention was Light feet, grateful heart. This week you pick one word (strong, smooth, free, steady, thankful, or your own) and run with it for an hour.

One word is short enough to hold onto when you're tired. A sentence falls apart at mile three. A word doesn't.

The run:
• Choose your word before the pace picks up
• Match it to your footsteps: say it on every left foot
• Find something in nature that matches your word, and be thankful for it
• When it gets hard, shrink the world down to the word on the next step
• Long quiet stretches with music, then a cool-down

Run somewhere safe and keep one ear open to traffic and the world around you.

Why it works: in a randomized study, cyclists who trained with motivational self-talk lasted longer at a hard effort and rated it as feeling easier (Blanchfield et al., 2014). One word is the simplest version of that self-talk.
