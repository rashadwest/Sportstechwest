# walkingIQ: 5-4-3-2-1 Reset Walk (1-hour guided walk, week 2)
# Same 5-4-3-2-1 senses tool, aimed at stress: the walk after a long day, a hard practice or a bad game.
# One segment per paragraph, 5 seconds of quiet after each. "[walk N]" = music-only stretch (scaled to fill 60:00).

This is walkingIQ. A reset walk. For after a long day, a tough practice, or a game that didn't go your way.

Walk somewhere safe, eyes up, one ear open.

Last week we learned five, four, three, two, one. Five things you see, four you touch, three you hear, two you smell, one you taste.

This week we use it the way athletes really need it. To reset when your head is loud.

First, let the day out. Walk a little faster for a minute if you need to. Let the breath be heavy. That's okay.
[walk 60]

Now slow it down a notch. Long breath out through the mouth. Let your shoulders fall.
[walk 45]

Name what you're carrying, just once. A mistake. A conversation. A score. Say it in your head. Then set it down. You can pick it back up later if you need to.
[walk 60]

Round one. Five things you see. Make them small and specific. Not just a tree. The shape of one leaf. The color of one door.
[walk 50]

Four things you can touch. Your heel hitting the ground. Your hands. The breeze. Your breath moving in your chest.
[walk 45]

Three things you hear. Two things you smell. One thing you taste.
[walk 60]

The day happened. And right now, I am walking. Both are true.
[walk 90]

When your mind replays the mistake, that's normal. Your brain is trying to protect you. Thank it, then hand it something else to do. Five things you see.
[walk 90]

I am more than one moment.
[walk 90]

Round two. Five sounds this time, instead of three. Reach for the quiet ones.
[walk 60]

Four textures you can feel. Three things in the distance you can see. Two smells. One taste.
[walk 60]

Mix it up like this when it starts to feel automatic. Keep your senses working, and your mind will stay where you are.

I let go of what I can't change.
[walk 120]

Quiet stretch. Just walk. If the day creeps back in, start a round.
[walk 150]

Here's the athlete's version. After a bad possession, you don't have an hour. You have one breath. One thing you see, one thing you hear. Then next play.

Practice the short version now. One thing you see. One thing you hear. Breathe out. Next step.
[walk 90]

Next play. Next step. Next breath.
[walk 120]

Round three. Slow. A full breath on each thing.
[walk 60]

Five you see. Four you touch. Three you hear. Two you smell. One you taste.
[walk 90]

I learn from the day. I don't live in it.
[walk 120]

Long quiet stretch. Let the music carry you for a while.
[walk 150]

Start to slow down. Easier steps. Long exhales.
[walk 75]

Notice how you feel now, compared to the start. Lighter. Quieter. That's not luck. That's a tool you used.

I am reset. I am ready for tomorrow.
[walk 45]

That's walkingIQ. Five, four, three, two, one. Your reset button, anytime you need it.
