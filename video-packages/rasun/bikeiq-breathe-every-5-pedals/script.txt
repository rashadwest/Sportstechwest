# bikeIQ: Breathe Every 5 Pedals (1-hour guided ride)
# Format: same as the walking/IQ sessions. One segment per paragraph, 5 seconds of quiet after each.
# A line "[ride N]" after a segment = about N seconds of music-only riding instead of the 5-second gap.
# The builder stretches or shrinks the ride blocks so the whole session lands on 60:00.
# Lines starting with # are notes and are never read aloud.

This is bikeIQ. One hour. One rhythm. Breathe every five pedals.

Ride somewhere safe for this. An indoor trainer, a bike path, a quiet road. Keep one ear open to the world around you.

Here's the whole idea. Your legs already keep time. We're going to let your breathing ride on that time.

Count one pedal every time your right foot reaches the bottom. Right foot down. One.

Breathe in for five pedals. Breathe out for five pedals. That's it. That's the whole session.

Start easy. No pushing yet. Just find the bottom of each right pedal and start counting.
[ride 45]

Now add the breath. In, for five right-foot pedals. Out, for five right-foot pedals.

In through the nose if you can. Out through the mouth, slow and long.

If five feels short, your cadence is quick. If five feels long, your cadence is slow. Either way, the pedals set the pace, not the clock.

Take the next ten breaths on your own. Count the pedals. Let the breath follow.
[ride 90]

Good. Notice what happened. Your mind had one job, and the noise got quieter.

I breathe on rhythm. I ride on rhythm.
[ride 60]

Relax your hands on the bars. Drop your shoulders away from your ears.

Your breath is the steering wheel for your nervous system. Slow, even breaths tell your body you're safe, so your legs can work.

Keep it going. In for five. Out for five.
[ride 90]

I am steady. Every pedal is a rep. Every breath is a choice.
[ride 60]

Check your cadence. Smooth circles, not stomps. Push through the front, pull through the bottom.

If you lose the count, don't restart the whole ride. Just wait for the next right foot at the bottom, and pick it back up at one.
[ride 90]

That's the skill. Not perfect counting. Coming back.

I come back to the rhythm. Every time.
[ride 75]

Let's build a little. Add some pressure to the pedals. Same cadence. Same breath. In for five. Out for five.
[ride 90]

As the effort rises, the breath wants to rush. Don't let it. The pedals stay on beat, so the breath stays on beat.

I control the pace. The pace does not control me.
[ride 90]

Make the exhale the strong part. Push the air out over all five pedals, all the way to the end.

A full exhale makes room for a full inhale. You don't have to pull air in. Just empty, and let it come back.
[ride 90]

I am strong in the middle of the ride. Not just at the start.
[ride 75]

Settle your face. Loose jaw. Soft eyes. Effort lives in the legs, not the face.
[ride 90]

Now picture a climb. Shift so the pedals get heavier. Your cadence drops a little, so each five pedals takes a little longer. Let the breath stretch with it.
[ride 90]

If five pedals is too long to hold right now, go to four. In for four, out for four. It's still rhythm. Come back to five when you can.

I can be uncomfortable and still be in control.
[ride 90]

Climbs don't get won at the top. They get won one breath at a time, right here.
[ride 90]

Keep the rhythm honest. In for five. Out for five. Right foot. Right foot. Right foot.

I keep my rhythm when it gets hard.
[ride 90]

Crest the hill. Shift back. Let the pedals spin lighter. Keep the count.
[ride 75]

Feel how fast your breath settles when the count is already there. That's the payoff. You trained it, so it's ready.
[ride 90]

I recover on purpose.
[ride 75]

Cruising now. Easy pressure. Five in. Five out. Let the miles pass under you.
[ride 120]

This rhythm goes with you. Off the bike, before a big moment, five slow counts in and five slow counts out does the same thing.

I trust my breath. I trust my legs.
[ride 120]

Notice what's around you. The road. The air. The sound of the wheels. Stay with the ride you're in.
[ride 120]

I am exactly where I need to be.
[ride 120]

One more steady block. No new instructions. Just the count.
[ride 150]

Still with me. In for five. Out for five. Good.

I finish what I start.
[ride 120]

Let's bring it down. Ease off the pressure. Keep spinning light.

Stretch the breath one more time. In for five. Out for six if it feels good. Long, slow, all the way out.
[ride 75]

Your heart rate is coming down because you told it to. That's a skill you just practiced for an hour.

I breathe on rhythm. I ride on rhythm. I am steady.
[ride 60]

Take three more breaths on the count, then let the counting go and just ride.
[ride 45]

That's bikeIQ. Breathe every five pedals. Take it on your next ride, and the one after that.
